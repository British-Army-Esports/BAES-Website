The 2026 BFBS Pro League wrapped up with its LAN final on 27 September, and British Army Esports came
away with results across all three games.

## League of Legends: Lions take the win

Earlier in the tournament, the BAES Lions took first place in League of Legends, defeating Real Humans
to win the title.

## F1 25: Army drivers 1st, 2nd and 4th

## Rocket League: a final decided in overtime

Three BAES teams made the Rocket League playoffs at the LAN: the Buzzards, Falcons and Kestrels. The
Buzzards knocked out the Falcons, while the RAF Typhoons knocked out the Kestrels.

<figure>
  <img src="/photos/news/bfbs-pro-league-2026/rl-typhoons-vs-falcons.jpg" alt="RAF Typhoons versus BAES Falcons on the main stage" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>RAF Typhoons vs BAES Falcons.</figcaption>
</figure>

The final was a nail-biter. The BAES Buzzards met S.E.A Dragons, a Civil Servant and Service Families
team, with almost every game going to sudden-death overtime. The Dragons just edged it, 4–2.

<figure>
  <img src="/photos/news/bfbs-pro-league-2026/rl-final-buzzards-vs-dragons.jpg" alt="The Rocket League final, S.E.A Dragons versus BAES Buzzards, on the main stage" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>The final: S.E.A Dragons vs BAES Buzzards.</figcaption>
</figure>

Bluey (BAES Buzzards) was named MVP, winning a BFBS gaming chair.
