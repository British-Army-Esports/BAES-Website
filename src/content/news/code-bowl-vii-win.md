British Army Esports took the trophy at the Call of Duty Endowment (C.O.D.E.) Bowl VII, presented
by USAA, streamed live from San Diego Race Weekend on 20 June 2026 — the first time the Army has
won the event.

US, UK, and Canadian military esports teams competed alongside top Call of Duty creators in
*Call of Duty: Warzone*. Previous winners were the RAF (2025 and 2022), US Space Force (2020),
Canadian Armed Forces (2023), and the US Army (2024).

The C.O.D.E. Bowl raises money for veteran employment — a competitive fixture with a purpose
beyond the result on the day.
