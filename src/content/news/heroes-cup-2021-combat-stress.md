The Heroes Cup, the British Army's annual esports competition, raised £1,857 for Combat Stress, a
charity providing specialist mental health treatment to veterans.

Teams competed across CS:GO, League of Legends and Rocket League, with the grand finals held on
13 December 2021 at Overworld Studios in Camberley and streamed live.
