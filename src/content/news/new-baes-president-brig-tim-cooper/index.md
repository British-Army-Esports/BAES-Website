---
title: "Brigadier Tim Cooper Becomes President of British Army Esports"
date: 2026-10-13
summary: "The officer who built Royal Signals esports from the ground up takes on the top job in Army esports."
---

British Army Esports has a new President. Brigadier Tim Cooper took up the role during the
International Defence Esports Games in Sunderland.

He brings a proven record of growing the game from the grassroots. As President of
[Royal Signals](/corps/rs) Esports, he took the sport at Corps level from almost nothing to monthly
events at [Blandford Camp](/locations/blandford-camp), the home of Royal Signals esports, including
regular show matches against other Corps.

Thanks to that grassroots work, Royal Signals players now feature heavily in both Army and UK Armed
Forces squads.
