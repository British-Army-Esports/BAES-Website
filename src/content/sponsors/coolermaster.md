Cooler Master is a computer hardware manufacturer, producing PC cases, cooling, power supplies,
and gaming peripherals.

Cooler Master has partnered with British Army Esports since summer 2025, supporting the
competitive team with PC cases for our gaming rigs. You'll see their branding on our Army jerseys
and Twitch channels. Serving personnel can also access a Cooler Master discount by registering on
their website with an MOD or Army email address.
