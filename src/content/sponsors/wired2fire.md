Wired2Fire is a UK custom gaming PC and workstation builder, hand-building systems in the UK
since 2003.

Wired2Fire has partnered with British Army Esports since summer 2025, supporting the competitive
team with components for our gaming rigs. You'll see their branding on our Army jerseys and Twitch
channels. Serving personnel can also access a Wired2Fire discount by registering on their website
with an MOD or Army email address.
